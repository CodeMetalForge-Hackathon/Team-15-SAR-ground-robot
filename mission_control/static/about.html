02 / THE MISSION LOGIC

A clue becomes a next step.

The demo concept, from first signal to operator report.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A phone or wearable provides a radio clue. Enrolled beacons can be authenticated; nearby signals remain unverified.

  2. 02

    Search

    The Mission view animates the search story. A separate planning sandbox combines a shared probability map with signal and thermal models.

  3. 03

    Look closer

    The ground vehicle brings the camera closer. Person and phone boxes add visual observations to the operator’s picture.

  4. 04

    Make the call

    The operator reviews the evidence and approves a bounded action, a stop, or a landing request.

  5. 05

    Report & reassess

    The demo animation records the scripted contact and condition, then returns or searches the next area.

03 / UNDER THE HOOD

A small stack, with distinct jobs.

01

Authenticated radio

BLE advertisements → ESP32 → USB serial → verification. Rotating identifiers, HMAC-SHA256, freshness checks, and replay state establish an enrolled beacon’s current token.

BLEESP32-C5HMAC-SHA256USB serial

RSSI is signal strength. It does not provide a measured bearing or camera identity.

02

Independent video & inference

Normal camera frames keep flowing while the detector works on the newest image. Boxes update between observations and expire when stale.

OpenCVPyTorch / torchvisionSSDLiteFaster R-CNN

Custom Crazyflie / RoverC classes require labeled captures and a validated checkpoint.

03

Search under a budget

MCTS evaluates useful next looks. A* supplies routes and return costs. Shared search belief and allocated footprints reduce repeated work across virtual agents.

Local operator control

The browser talks to a local Python coordinator. Role tokens, per-action approvals, device heartbeats, and expiring leases govern command delivery.

ROADMAP FROM THE BRIEF

The larger deployment concept

  • A mothership UAV providing launch, charging, and a shared origin for smaller scouts.
  • Robust localization and coordinated search in GPS-denied or no-service areas.
  • Wearable heart-rate integration, human re-identification, obstacle avoidance, and injury assessment research.

Hardware compatibility and field performance need testing on the actual vehicles. A camera box is a visual observation; condition and response decisions remain with the operator.
